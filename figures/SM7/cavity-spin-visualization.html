The 17 exponents are estimated live with Benettin's method — co-evolving the tangent vectors using the analytic Jacobian and re-orthonormalising each step (Gram–Schmidt). Because only the cavity dissipates, the spectrum must satisfy Σλ = −κ = −0.05 exactly, regardless of ε — a built-in check. Estimates refine over ~20–30 s of running. λ(t)=1 on the first half of each period, 0 on the second. Populations pⱼ are drawn per trajectory from a Multinomial(N, ¼) and held fixed (ṗ=0).
