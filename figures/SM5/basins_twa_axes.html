The two variables chosen for the axes are swept over the grid; the remaining three are held at the values set under Initial state. Each grid point is integrated to t = 15·2π and the pixel is blue where sign S_x > 0, red where it is negative. TWA points are drawn around the initial state: the quadratures Re[a], Im[a] from a Gaussian of width 1/(2√N), the spin from N binomial flips ±1 in the two directions transverse to S(0), so the cloud shrinks as 1/√N. With “fluctuate only the two plotted variables” ticked, noise in the other three is suppressed, so every sample really does lie in the plane shown; untick it for the full coherent-state ensemble, in which the dots are a projection and the colour lookup is only approximate.
